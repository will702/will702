## Selected systems

### [mlx-vision](https://github.com/will702/mlx-vision)

**Speculative OCR decoding for Apple Silicon.** An MLX toolkit for DeepSeek-OCR-2, GLM-OCR, and Unlimited-OCR with training-free and neural draft modes, benchmarking, a Python API, and an OpenAI-compatible server.

`Python` `MLX` `Vision-Language Models` `Speculative Decoding`

---

### [StockPro](https://ipo-tracker.site/)

**A production research terminal for Indonesian equities.** Connects IDX data pipelines, technical and fundamental analysis, broker-flow research, screeners, portfolio tooling, paper trading, and AI-assisted workflows.

[Live product](https://ipo-tracker.site/) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.will702.uwtrackermobile)

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Quantitative Finance`

---

### [QuakeSense](https://github.com/will702/Quakesense.swiftpm)

**An earthquake-survival simulation that teaches through physical interaction.** A two-minute Swift Playground combining SpriteKit physics, motion controls, procedural audio, seismic haptics, accessible feedback, and a scored debrief.

`SwiftUI` `SpriteKit` `Core Haptics` `Core Motion`

---

### [statement-to-tikz](https://github.com/will702/statement-to-tikz)

**Constraint-solved diagrams from olympiad geometry statements.** Converts text into a GeometryIR, solves supported constraints with SciPy, emits standalone TikZ, and reports whether the result is exact or a schematic fallback.

`Python` `SciPy` `TikZ` `Constraint Solving`

---

### [Interactive ML](https://interactive-ml-drab.vercel.app/)

**A browser laboratory for machine-learning intuition.** Makes optimization, clustering, dimensionality reduction, and decision boundaries directly manipulable through real-time visual experiments.

[Live lab](https://interactive-ml-drab.vercel.app/) · [Source](https://github.com/will702/interactive-ml)

`React` `TypeScript` `Data Visualization` `Interactive Learning`

---
